About EasySplit

EasySplit (夾錢易) started in Hong Kong, where “加一” — the 10% service charge — makes every group dinner a small maths puzzle. It grew into a calculator for meals, gatherings and trips anywhere in the world.

It knows the usual service charges, taxes and tipping customs of 30+ countries, handles any currency, and works out the fewest transfers to settle up.

Principles

Free to use and ad-free. No sign-up. Works offline. Your data stays on your device unless you choose to share a group live.

Support

EasySplit is a side project. If it helps you and your friends, you can support it on Ko-fi: https://ko-fi.com/beyond01hk

Feedback

Found a wrong tax rate or a bug? Tell us at https://github.com/bill2712/-EasySplit-HK-/issues.
