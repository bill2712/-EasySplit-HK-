Split the bill with friends — service charge, tax and tip included

EasySplit is a free bill-splitting calculator for meals, gatherings and trips. Enter the bill, choose your country, and it adds the local service charge, tax and tip, then shows exactly what each person pays.

Open the calculator

How to split a bill in 3 steps

  1. Enter the bill amount, or add each dish and tap who shared it.
  2. Check the service charge, tax and tip — local defaults are filled in for 30+ countries.
  3. Share the result to your group chat, or save it to a group to settle up later.

Frequently asked questions

How do I split a bill with a 10% service charge?

Enter the food and drink total, keep the service charge at 10% (set automatically for Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia and others) and choose how many people. EasySplit adds the charge and divides it, so each share includes its part of the service charge.

How much should I tip in different countries?

It depends on where you are: in the US 18–22% is normal, in Canada 15–20%, in much of Europe you round up, and in Japan, Korea and Hong Kong tipping isn’t expected. The tipping guide lists the usual service charge, tax and tip for 30+ countries.

Can I split trip expenses in different currencies?

Yes. Log each expense in the currency you paid in. EasySplit fetches live exchange rates (you can edit them) and converts everything to the group’s main currency for the final balances.

How does EasySplit keep the number of transfers low?

It adds up what everyone paid and consumed, nets it out, and suggests the fewest payments that settle everyone. You can switch this off to see who owes whom directly.

What about couples, birthdays and people who didn’t drink?

Mark a couple or family as “settled together” so they pay as one. Tap 🎂 to treat the birthday person. Split by item, by shares or by exact amounts when people had different things.

Is it free? Where is my data stored?

EasySplit is free, needs no account and works offline. Your groups are stored only on your device; share a link or QR code when friends need a copy. If it helps you, you can support the project on Ko-fi.
